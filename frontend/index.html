Methodology

Evidence before conclusions.

How the case queue and investigation evidence are produced.

Source-native time

All events use IBM simulation steps. We do not invent timestamps or duration in minutes.

Priority formula

context_evidence_v2_heuristic: P = 100 × (0.45M + 0.35A + 0.20C). M is maximum active-alert severity; A is the strongest focus-account peer/history anomaly; C is nonredundant support across detector families after overlap discounts. Weighted factor points are rounded to two decimals before summing.

Counterfactual review

Exclude one transaction or a flagged pattern’s transfers temporarily, then recalculate alert status and score. Case membership, time bounds, and the peer reference distribution stay fixed.

Validation limits

This is a heuristic investigation-priority index, not a fraud probability. Account-level labels are withheld, so predictive accuracy has not been measured.
